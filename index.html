The resort

Banff Sunshine Village is absurd.

It sits on the Continental Divide, straddling the Alberta–British Columbia border, inside a UNESCO World Heritage national park. You ride a 15–20 minute gondola just to reach the village where the lifts start. It's the highest-elevation resort in the Canadian Rockies, and the snow is all natural.

Once in a lifetime

The story you'll still be telling at 60.

  1. 01

    Hot springs at -15°C

    Banff Upper Hot Springs: 1,585 m up, 38°C water, steam rolling off into frozen air, mountains in every direction. Après-ski does not get better than this.

  2. 02

    Skate across Lake Louise

    The lake freezes into a rink with the Victoria Glacier as the backdrop and a castle-hotel on the shore. It's one of the most photographed places on Earth and we'll be standing in the middle of it.

  3. 03

    Walk through a frozen canyon

    Johnston Canyon in winter: catwalks bolted to canyon walls, waterfalls frozen solid into blue ice. Ice cleats on, cameras out.

  4. 04

    Maybe, the northern lights

    Banff sits under some of the darkest skies in North America and January nights are long. No promises, but if the forecast lights up, we're driving to Lake Minnewanka at midnight.

  5. 05

    Elk in the street

    Banff is a town inside a national park. Elk wander past the coffee shops. You will absolutely stop traffic to take a photo. Everyone does.

  6. 06

    Ring in 30 at altitude

    A birthday toast at the top of a 2,730 m peak with people I actually like. That's the whole plan. That's the only plan.

Currency

Your dollar is bigger here.

Canada runs on the Canadian dollar (CAD, the “loonie”). It has traded well below the US dollar for years. Every price tag you see is quietly discounted.

A CA$179 lift ticket is really US$131. A CA$9 après beer is US$6.57. A CA$45 dinner is US$32.85.

  • 💳
    Tap-to-pay everywhere. Canada is more card-friendly than the US. You don't need to touch an ATM all weekend.
  • 🚫
    Bring a no-foreign-transaction-fee card. Most travel cards (Chase Sapphire, Capital One, Amex Platinum/Gold) are fine. Skip the 3% fee on a basic card.
  • 🧾
    Alberta has the lowest sales tax in Canada. Just 5% GST and no provincial sales tax. Prices on menus are pre-tax, like home.
  • 🫶
    Tipping works like the US. 15–20% at restaurants and bars. Your card terminal will ask.
  • 📱
    Phone. Most US plans include Canada roaming (T-Mobile, Verizon, AT&T travel plans). Check yours before you fly.

Your objections, handled

“But—”

I've never snowboarded or skied.

Perfect. Sunshine's beginner area on Mt. Standish is wide and forgiving, and group lessons run every morning. Rent everything at the village. Day one you'll fall. Day two you'll ride. Day three you'll be insufferable about it.

Isn't it brutally cold?

It's cold. January at Sunshine is typically -8°C to -18°C (18°F to 0°F), colder on storm days. Solution: layers, a good shell, mittens over gloves, hand warmers, a neck gaiter. The gondola, lodges, and hot springs are all heated. Cold means the snow stays perfect.

I don't have gear.

Rent a full setup (board/skis, boots, helmet) at the base or in Banff town. Jackets and pants are rentable too. You need to bring base layers, gloves, socks, and goggles or sunglasses.

Do I need a passport or visa?

You need a valid passport to fly into Canada. US citizens: no visa, no eTA. US green card holders: passport plus green card, no eTA. Other passports: check Canada's eTA/visa requirements. It's a 5-minute online form for most.

How do we get from Calgary to Banff?

Rent an SUV together (about 90 minutes on a plowed four-lane highway), or take the Banff Airporter shuttle from the terminal. Sunshine's gondola base is 15 minutes past Banff town with free parking and a free shuttle from town.

Where are we staying?

Depends on headcount. Options: a big Airbnb or hotel in Banff town (restaurants, bars, hot springs nearby), or the Sunshine Mountain Lodge, Banff's only ski-in/ski-out hotel, which is on the mountain itself. Tell me you're in and I'll lock it.

What if I can only do part of it?

Come for Saturday and Sunday. Fly in Friday night, out Sunday night. Still zero PTO, still two full days on snow, still the birthday dinner.
